# eccofs-model-currents-repo

The currents of NOAA's East Coast Community Ocean Forecast System (ECCOFS), as
gridded data at 0.04° for the oceansensing.org ocean map: at the surface, at
50 m, averaged over the top 200, 350 and 1000 m, at the bottom, and the day's
mean at each of 48 depths. Its sibling, `eccofs-model-fields-repo`, publishes
the model's scalar fields.

Updated every three hours.
